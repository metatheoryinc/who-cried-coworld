# Full role catalog and daytime private coordination

Goal: port all 11 source role types before another paid LLM playtest.
Architecture: retain authoritative Session and typed seat observations. Add a
configurable nine-seat role deck (default stays the original five role types).
Use deterministic private discussion windows for living Wolves and Nobles before
public discussion and between discussion rounds. Preserve fixed public timing.
Tech stack: TypeScript, Zod, Vitest, current Node/Coworld runtime.

Source: tofu-tech/apps/@hotpot-arcade/packages/games/mafia/src/shared/types/player-roles.ts
and server/machine/handlers/night-handler.ts, check-game-over.ts, plus noble
knowledge/private-channel routing. Marketing Jester is named Trickster in game.

Implementation sequence:
1. Role catalog, configurable deck validation, seeded assignment, solo result.
2. Chef jail, Track Reader check, Dairy Maid inform, Priest visits; deterministic
   blocking order and private result schemas. Noble identity knowledge.
3. Private Wolf and Noble day exchanges, night channel schedule, legal fallbacks,
   audience filtering, and bounded episode budget.
4. Policy prompts, shared replay fold/UI labels, manifest regeneration.
5. Rules/privacy/session tests, full checks and free scripted coverage. No paid
   LLM run until this expanded role work is complete and reviewed.

Semantics: Alchemist block first; Chef jail then Guard protection; faction kill;
Dairy Maid, Seer, Track Reader, Priest results only for surviving actors. Seer
reports wolves/non-wolves, including solo as non-wolf. Noble identities are
mutually known. Trickster wins immediately on majority vote elimination, before
faction checks; a night death does not win. Wolf parity counts every non-wolf.
NewD3 supersedes the earlier kill tally: wolves nominate a target and a specific
killer. Blocking that killer stops the entire kill; other nominations do not
bypass the block. Priest sees the selected killer's visit, not every nomination.
Conflicting nominations are resolved deterministically before checking blocks.
