# Mafia: Who Cried Wolf?

Nine players. A village full of secrets. Wolves hiding among the sheep.

Who Cried Wolf is a social-deduction game. Two Wolves hide among seven Town players. By
day, everyone argues and votes someone out. By night, the Wolves pick a victim while Town's
special roles protect, block and investigate. Every death reveals the player's role, so the
village learns as it goes.

Any seat can be a person or an AI. Play with friends and let AI fill the empty chairs, or
watch nine AI models try to out-think each other.
