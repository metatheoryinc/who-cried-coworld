# Strategy tips

A few habits that win more games. None of them are rules.

## For everyone

- **Deaths are evidence.** Every death reveals a role. Check who pushed for a Town player
  who got voted out, and who defended a Wolf.
- **Votes are public; reasons aren't.** The journal keeps every ballot. Players who switch
  votes at the last second, or always vote with the crowd, are worth a closer look.
- **Work out the setup.** There are only nine setups (see [Rules and roles](rules-and-roles)).
  A revealed or claimed role rules several of them out, and tells you which power roles
  might still be hiding.
- **Skipping is a real choice.** No majority means nobody dies. With a weak case, a skip can
  be better than a coin-flip on a Town player, because every Town death brings Wolves
  closer to parity.

## As Town

- **Sheep:** your vote is your power. Ask specific questions ("Who would you vote, and
  why?") and push quiet players to commit.
- **Seer:** a Wolf result is powerful but makes you a target. Consider waiting until you
  have a result worth dying for, or until the Guard can cover you.
- **Guard / Chef:** protect whoever the Wolves most want dead: a claimed Seer, or the
  player leading the case against the Wolves.
- **Dairy Maid:** your milk proves you're Town to one player. Pick someone influential, and
  they can vouch for you.
- **Priest:** track a suspect. If they visited the player who died, you've probably found
  the Wolf holding the knife.
- **Nobles:** you know each other are Town, a solid core to build on. Be careful about
  revealing it: Wolves may pick you off.

## As a Wolf

- **Blend in.** Take part, hold opinions, vote. Silent players draw suspicion.
- **Agree before the timer ends.** Place your Kill target and Knife stamps early; your
  partner sees your drafts. A split vote is broken at random.
- **Hunt power roles.** A claimed Seer or a suspected Guard is usually the best kill.
- **Alchemist:** block whoever is most likely to protect or investigate the kill target.
- **Track Reader:** find the power roles, then kill them or discredit them by day.
- **Watch the count.** You win at parity. A Town vote-out plus a night kill each day gets
  there quickly; don't take risks you don't need to.

## Playing with AI

- Address AI players by name; the host prioritizes replies to people.
- AI players read the whole transcript and track votes carefully. A consistent story
  matters more than a clever one.
- Their confessionals and suspicion reports appear in the replay afterwards. It's a
  great way to see what they actually believed.
